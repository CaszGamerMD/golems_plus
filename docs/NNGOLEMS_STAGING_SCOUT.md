# Staging scout validation: first experimental A/B was inconclusive
The prior Farm Demo staging A/B produced 312 vs 278 correct cells but **zero `NN FRONTIER STAGE START` events**. Therefore its difference was not a real staging test. The actual root cause was recovery staffing: repeated failed crew returns reduced a large layer to a single builder, who was already near the frontier, while unloaded builders farther away were all sidelined. No worker that could benefit from a short ground-stage route was eligible to initiate one.

New opt-in scope GOLEMS_NN_FRONTIER_STAGING=true: after two exhausted recoveries and a further no-placement timeout, recruit at most one demonstrably unloaded, grounded, empty-pouched OnSite worker to the reduced roster for the current layer. Preserve the original worker, inventories, claims and routes. The recruited scout can attempt bounded, short, collision-checked waypoints toward far physical tasks or claim real executable work normally. If no safe scout is present, log the reason and leave the original project-yield recovery unchanged. The next A/B checks `[NN STAGE SCOUT]` and `[NN FRONTIER STAGE START]` explicitly; a no-activation run fails the experiment rather than claiming improvement. Both flags remain off by default and main is untouched.
